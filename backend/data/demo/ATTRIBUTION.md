# Attribution: Sidekick demo-mode photos

These photos are public engine bays used for a UX demo. None of them is Zeke's truck. Each was resized (long side at most 1600 px, JPEG q85); no other edits.
The part polygons and `demo_state` values in `demo_bay_photos.json` are ours (scripted demo data drawn on top of the photos), not part of the original works.
